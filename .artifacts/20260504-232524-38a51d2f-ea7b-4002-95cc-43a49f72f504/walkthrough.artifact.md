## Workout Plan Tracking
The **Workout Plan** feature allows users to build and manage their personal exercise routines.

- **Exercise Details**: Users can add exercises with specific metrics:
  - Exercise Name
  - Workout Type (Strength, Cardio, Flexibility)
  - Muscle Group
  - Sets and Reps
  - Weight (kg)
  - Rest Time (seconds)
  - Duration (for Cardio)
- **Persistence**: Exercises are saved locally, ensuring the plan is retained even after closing the app.
